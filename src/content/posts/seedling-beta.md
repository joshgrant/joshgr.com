_After a 7 year hiatus, I'm finally watering Seedling. Why did it take so long?_

I checked the stats. 111,906 people have downloaded the app to date (yes, I regret not having an in-app purchase or two). Yet, I never updated the app. Why?

1. My first attempt was when a college student reached out, wanting to learn how to code. I started mentoring them, and we worked on a Seedling update. Then, after 4 months, they got a job. Mission accomplished? But I lost momentum when they left.
2. A year later, I tried re-writing the app with SwiftUI, but frankly, it wasn't mature enough then (and might still not be). It was so frustrating I abandoned the project.
3. I tried mentoring someone again, my nephew, and eventually he realized that "coding wasn't for him". Maybe I'm a bad mentor? No… that can't be it.
4. I tried again a few years later, with UIKit and CoreData, but then _I_ got a job and had to devote my energies elsewhere.

So now, after 7 years, I'm publishing an update. My dirty secret? Welcome to the New Age: I used Claude. Sketch MCP server with my comprehensive designs, old code as reference, a very long and detailed hand-written spec, and my "app framework example" project which has all of the best practices I use.

It took two days, and it's better than I could've imagined.

That's why it took so long. But also, _why now_?

It was this email I got, out of the blue:

> I love the app seedling and have been using it for a very long time it helps me through my day to day planning.
I was wondering if you can or have any plans to add a shortcut where we can jump to a certain date and month instead of having to press right loads of times at the top?
It would really help if possible!
Anyway just wanted to say this is a great app

That warmed my cold, brittle heart. How could I say no?

It was a good reminder that whatever you create leaves an impact on the world, no matter how small you think it is. And sometimes it's good to water your plants, every once in a while.
